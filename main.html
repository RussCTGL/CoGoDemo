Why CoGO works

A trust-first transportation coordination platform for students.

01

Student verification

Registration is limited to valid .edu email addresses, creating a closed campus-oriented network.

02

Driver Match

Students with cars can publish open seats, show vehicle details, and share transportation costs.

03

Rider Match

Students without cars can coordinate shared Uber or Lyft trips and divide fare costs.

Structured compatibility

Route, time, meeting point, optional gender preference, and trust scores all improve fit.

Built around institutional identity, not anonymous ride requests.

Closed student community

CoGO limits participation to verified university affiliates, creating a stronger sense of security than open consumer marketplaces.
