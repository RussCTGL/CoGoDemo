Student-exclusive ride coordination in Los Angeles

Match routes, split costs, and ride with verified students only.

CoGO is a student-only route-matching platform built for campus communities. Students join with a valid .edu email, enter where they are going, and get matched through two flows: Driver Match for students offering seats and Rider Match for students coordinating shared Uber or Lyft trips.

Why CoGO works

A trust-first transportation coordination platform for students.

01

Student verification

Registration is limited to valid .edu email addresses, creating a closed campus-oriented network that improves safety, trust, and accountability.

02

Driver Match

Students with cars can publish open seats, share transportation costs, and coordinate pickups with peers traveling in the same direction.

03

Rider Match

Students without cars can form shared Uber or Lyft groups, reduce individual fare costs, and align on time and meeting points before booking.

04

Structured compatibility filters

Users can match by route, departure time, meeting location, and optional gender preference to improve fit and simplify coordination.
